How SADEP reads a sentence

Your text is broken into tokens, turned into vectors, and passed through a bidirectional GRU that reads the sentence forward and backward at once. A classifier layer then scores it against six emotions and returns the strongest match with a confidence level.

01 Your sentence Raw text, tokenized and cleaned
›
02 Embedding Words mapped to vectors
›
03 BiGRU layer Reads context both ways
›
04 Classifier Scores all six emotions
›
05 Result Top emotion + confidence
